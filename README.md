An AI therapist's one-line diagnosis for make.fun's "Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis" bounty — a calm clinical verdict that trusts the patient's own late-night search history over their official file.

> "Your file says: 'fine.' Your 3 a.m. search history says: 'am I a bad person?' I'm siding with the search history."

## Why this line

The joke works because the therapist never raises its voice. It just reads the chart twice: once from the file, where the patient is doing fine, and once from the evidence only the patient knows exists — the 3 a.m. search history, the hour when nobody performs for anyone. Quoting the patient back to themselves ("am I a bad person?") turns their own words into the diagnosis, and the deadpan punchline — *I'm siding with the search history* — lands the laugh at the very end, after the setup has already felt completely reasonable. It's the line everyone recognises, because everyone has typed something at 3 a.m. they'd never say out loud.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
